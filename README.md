# Savings Strategy Modeller

A tool for comparing and modelling different savings strategies over time, with a focus on UK savings products such as Lifetime ISAs (LISAs) and Stocks & Shares ISAs.

The project aims to help users understand how different contribution levels, savings allocations, interest rates, investment returns and government bonuses affect long-term savings growth.

## Motivation

This project grew out of a personal interest in financial planning and a desire to better understand how different savings strategies could support long-term financial goals. In particular, I wanted to explore the trade-offs between products such as LISAs and ISAs, experiment with variable monthly contributions, and understand how different assumptions affect the time required to reach savings targets.

Rather than relying on static calculators, the aim is to build a flexible tool that allows different strategies and scenarios to be modelled and compared.

## Planned Features

- **Flexible contributions:** Model fixed or variable monthly contributions, including changes over time.
- **LISA modelling:** Account for the £4,000 annual contribution limit and 25% government bonus.
- **ISA comparisons:** Compare Cash ISAs and Stocks & Shares ISAs under different return assumptions.
- **Multiple savings strategies:** Test different allocations across savings accounts and investment products.
- **Growth projections:** Model compound interest, investment returns and contributions over configurable time horizons.
- **Scenario comparison:** Compare strategies side by side to identify the impact of different assumptions and decisions.
- **Visualisation:** Display projected balances, contributions, bonuses and returns over time.

## Objective

Build a flexible modelling tool that makes it easier to evaluate savings strategies, understand trade-offs and plan towards financial goals such as purchasing a property or building long-term wealth.
